# HW3 Analysis: Executive Changes vs. Earnings Announcements

The executive changes in my data come before earnings announcements more often than after them. This is because 22 of the 32 named events were filed before the nearest earnings 8-K, 8 were filed after, and 2 fell in the same week (the 3 compensation-only filings with no named person are left out of these counts). Walmart has the strongest pattern with 8 events before earnings and 2 in the same week, including Doug McMillon's retirement filed six days before the third-quarter FY26 results, and JPMorgan (4 before, 0 after) and NVIDIA (4 before, 2 after) lean the same way. Apple splits 5 before and 4 after, and Microsoft has 2 of its 3 board changes after earnings, so the pattern varies by company. Because most events sit 10 to 43 days from an earnings date and several are board seats tied to annual meeting cycles, the timing looks driven by each company's own calendar, and 32 events is too small a sample to show that companies time leadership news around earnings on purpose.
